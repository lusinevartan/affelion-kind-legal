Help with Kind.

Affelion LLC · Updated October 1, 2026

Get in touch

Email hello@affelion.com for help. You can ask about the app, purchases, privacy or product safety.

Tell us your app version, device type and a few words about the issue. Do not send private chats, health records, passwords or card details. We may ask for a store order reference if we need it to check a purchase.

What Kind can and cannot do

Kind offers ready-made words for day-to-day talks. It does not give medical, mental health or legal advice. It does not replace help from a trained expert.

Kind cannot spot, treat or prevent health problems. It cannot judge your safety, health or relationship. It cannot know what someone else means or promise a response or outcome. No health expert or other trained adviser checks your choices.

Do not put off expert help due to what you read in Kind. For health concerns, ask a trained health expert. If you face control, threats, stalking or abuse, seek a service trained to help with that issue. Do not rely on a stock message.

Urgent help

If you or someone else is in danger now, call your local emergency number. Kind and email support are not emergency services. Neither calls for help for you.

Find support can open a list of helplines on another site. It needs internet access. It does not replace emergency services. If someone checks your device, think about using a safer device.

When contact is not welcome

Do not use Kind to evade a block, a request for no contact or a legal restriction. Choose wording only if it fits the situation and contact is welcome and safe. You can always leave a message unsent.

If you are a teenager

If someone puts pressure on you, threatens you or makes you feel unsafe, think about talking to a trusted adult or a youth support service. Choose someone safe to approach. You do not need to find the perfect message to deal with an unsafe situation.

Do not email us your school, exact location, private chats or intimate images. Kind support helps with the app. It does not offer private mental health advice or emergency help.

What Copy does

Kind copies text; it does not send messages. If copying fails, the words remain available to select or retry. Your clipboard and any app where you paste text follow your device settings and that service's practices.

Buying and restoring access

Standard words are free to browse, edit and copy. This includes other wording options. Guided choices let you pick your goal, tone and length. You can try those choices free in this review build. Sales are off while the new offer is checked. No price or subscription has been approved for this build.

Use Restore my purchase with the store account you used to buy. Buying from one store does not mean you get access in another store or on the web. If a payment check stops, do not assume you were not charged. Check your store purchase history before you try again.

If your store needs a parent or guardian to approve a purchase, wait for them to approve it. Do not use someone else's way to pay without their okay. A request that is still pending is not a purchase. You do not have to buy anything to use the free words or Find support.

For Apple purchases, use Apple's purchase support. For Google Play purchases, use Google Play Help. Contact us if you cannot access content you paid for.

Your legal rights

This page does not take away rights or ways to seek redress that the law protects. Refunds, rights to cancel and purchase terms depend on the law that applies and the store used. We do not set a blanket “no refunds” rule here.

For iOS, Apple's standard app license applies under Apple's rules if no custom license is set. This page does not make you give up rights or agree to arbitration.

Language and where to use Kind

The app is in English. Picking a country for help does not change its language or mean it fits every country. Before adding a language, we must check the translated screens, words, safety guidance and purchase details.
