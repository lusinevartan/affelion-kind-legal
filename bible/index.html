Affelion Bible

Your prayers and reflections stay on your phone.

Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version

Affelion Bible runs fully offline. Nothing you write, read, or listen to is sent anywhere. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising SDK in this app.

Your journal and progress

Your reflections, which lessons you've finished, and your settings are stored only on your device. If your phone's own backup is turned on (iCloud or Google), that backup includes them, under your account and Apple's or Google's terms. We never receive a copy.

You can save or back up your journal as a file, restore it, or erase everything from About, privacy and support inside the app. Erasing can't be undone.

Reminders

If you choose a daily time, the reminder is scheduled by your phone itself. It carries no information about you and nothing is sent to us.

What we do not collect

No name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or device identifiers. No cookies. No tracking. We don't know what you pray about, how often you open the app, or what you believe. No profile of you exists, because none is built.

Links that leave the app

The support links (findahelpline.com, the 988 line in the US, and the International OCD Foundation) open outside Affelion Bible under their own privacy practices. The app doesn't record that you opened them.

Children's privacy

Affelion Bible is not directed at children under 13. Because no personal information is collected from any user, none is knowingly collected from a child either, consistent with COPPA.

If this changes

Any future change, such as an account, sync, or an analytics feature, gets this page updated first and the App Store and Google Play listings updated after.
