How Kind uses your data.

Updated October 9, 2026.

This notice describes Kind's current review build. New sales are off. Store data and support practices still need to be checked before public launch.

Kind and your data

Affelion LLC provides Kind. Its words are built into the app and work offline. There is no Kind account or ad software. Kind's own code does not track how you use it. Kind does not send your chosen situation or message text to us. Kind keeps no history of situations or wording.

On your device

Kind saves a few settings, such as whether you have seen the welcome or a prompt. A web version may save a purchase key and status. Native apps check access with the store. Your help country stays in this session. Kind may suggest it from your device language, not GPS. You can change it. Kind does not log help-link visits.

Local settings stay until cleared. Removing the app or its browser data usually clears them. Device backups and store records are separate. Kind cannot hide its use, your clipboard or device and browser records.

Copy puts words on your clipboard. It does not send them. Other apps, clipboard sync or device features may keep or share copied text under your settings. What you paste or send elsewhere follows that service's rules.

Help from the stores

New sales are off in this review build. Apple or Google handles native prices, access checks, purchases and restores. Store checks may need internet access, even when new sales are off. Requests do not include your chosen situation or message text. We do not receive your card details. Stores may give us sales, financial and platform reports. They set their own rules for keeping purchase records.

Store code may send data on how it runs. We still need to check the full data flow and the store forms before launch.

Web checkout is off. If web purchase checks are enabled, your key goes to Gumroad to check it.

Help links and websites

Help links open only when you choose them. Those sites and our page hosts may receive your IP address and browser details to serve and protect their sites. Their own privacy rules apply. Our legal site uses GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and files. Your email provider and Google, which hosts our support mail, process the message to deliver it. You can contact us for app, purchase or privacy questions, or to report a safety issue.

Do not send private chats, health records, passwords or card details. Support is not an emergency service or clinical care.

Access, retention and deletion details for support still need to be checked before launch. This notice does not promise a fixed deletion date. You can ask about your message or make a privacy request at the address below.

Your rights and age

Based on the law that applies, you may ask to see, copy, correct or delete your data, limit or object to its use, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy regulator. Contact hello@affelion.com. We may need to check that a request is yours. For data held by a store or other service, contact it too.

Kind does not ask for your birth date or ID. A store age rating does not mean Kind checks your age.

Changes

We will update this policy when our data practices change. Where the law requires notice or consent, we will give notice or ask first.
