Affelion Kind

What stays on your phone, stated plainly.

Last updated 16 August 2026

The short version

Kind runs fully offline. Nothing you type or select is sent anywhere. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising SDK in this app.

What Kind does with what you type

Every situation, principle, and pre-written message lives inside the app. Anything you personalize, or copy to send, stays on your device only. Nothing is logged, transmitted, or shared.

Purchases

The one optional in-app purchase runs through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details, and we keep no purchase records of our own.

What we do not collect

No name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or device identifiers. No cookies. No tracking. No profile of you exists, because none is built.

The safety floor

The in-app helpline link opens findahelpline.com, outside Kind, under its own privacy practices. Kind does not record that you opened it and does not know where you are. Your selected country is held in memory only, gone the moment you close the app.

Children's privacy

Kind is rated 12+ and is not directed at children. Because no personal information is collected from any user, none is knowingly collected from a child either, consistent with COPPA.

If this changes

Any future change, such as an account or an analytics feature, gets this page updated first and the App Store listing updated after.
