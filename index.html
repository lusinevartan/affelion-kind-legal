Your privacy, stated plainly.

Updated October 2, 2026. Release policy draft for the current app; not yet approved for publication.

Who provides Kind

Affelion LLC provides Affelion Kind. Contact hello@affelion.com for privacy questions or requests.

The short version

Kind's prewritten message library works offline. This version has no Kind account, advertising SDK or in-app analytics. It does not send your selected situation or message text to Affelion.

That does not mean your phone stores nothing or that every feature works without internet. Purchases use store services. A helpline opens an outside website. Email support and visits to our public pages involve separate data handling, explained below.

Messages and settings on your device

The situations, principles and prewritten messages are bundled with the app. Kind does not save a history of the situations or messages you choose.

Kind stores a few local settings, including whether you have seen the welcome or a prompt. In a web version using license verification, a purchase key and purchase-status settings may also be stored locally. The native app checks access with the store rather than trusting a web license key.

The optional help-country selection is held in memory, not saved as a setting. A country may initially be suggested from device language settings, not GPS. You can correct it.

Local settings remain until cleared by the app, browser or device. Removing the app or clearing its browser data generally removes local app data; device backups, store records and operating-system behavior are outside Kind's control.

Clipboard and other apps

Copy message and Copy for myself ask your device to put that wording on its clipboard. Kind does not send the message for you. Other apps, clipboard synchronization or device features may access or retain copied text under your settings.

Anything you paste or send elsewhere is handled by that app or service. Kind cannot hide its installation, your clipboard, browser history, device backups or operating-system records.

Stores and purchases

Apple or Google handles native prices, purchases and restorations through its store services. Your selected situation and message text are not included in those purchase requests. Affelion does not receive your payment-card details through Kind.

Stores may provide Affelion with sales, financial and platform reports under their terms. This is separate from tracking your choices inside Kind. Purchase records held by a store follow that store's privacy and retention practices.

If web license verification is enabled, the key you enter is sent to Gumroad to check the purchase. Web checkout is not enabled in the current review version.

External help and public pages

The helpline directory opens only when you choose its link. The destination can receive information about your visit, such as your IP address and browser information, under its own practices. Kind does not save a record of opening that link or of your chosen help country.

Our public support and privacy pages are hosted separately from the native app. Hosting providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to serve and protect those pages. A website visit is not covered by a promise of “nothing leaves your device.”

The public legal site currently uses GitHub Pages. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

If you contact us

We receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, handle privacy or safety reports, and meet applicable legal obligations. Email and hosting providers process information needed to provide those services.

Please do not send private relationship conversations, health records, passwords or payment-card details. Support is not monitored as an emergency service and does not provide clinical care.

Support information is retained only as needed for the request, follow-up, security or applicable legal requirements. You may ask us to delete it; we will explain any information we must retain. No automatic deletion deadline is promised here.

Your requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to certain uses, obtain a copy, withdraw consent where consent is used, or complain to a privacy regulator.

Email hello@affelion.com with your request. We may need limited information to verify that it concerns you. We cannot retrieve a history of your selected messages from an Affelion account because this version does not create one. For records held independently by Apple, Google or another service, contact that service as well.

Proposed audience and availability

The current proposal is to offer Kind to adults in selected countries. The age threshold, available countries, store rating and any age-related safeguards remain undecided pending legal review and must be finalized before release. This draft does not establish an adults-only launch or claim that access is restricted by age or country.

This version does not ask for a birth date or identity document, and an app-store rating is not a claim that Kind verifies everyone's age. We are not adding an account, advertising or relationship profiling to identify younger users.

Before release in any selected country, this policy must be completed against the confirmed support providers, retention practices, handling of information received from younger users, international processing arrangements and applicable regional requirements. This draft is not a claim of global or children's-privacy compliance.

Changes

If the app or our data practices change, we will update the relevant disclosures. Where notice or consent is required, we will provide it before making the change. New account, analytics or cloud features require a fresh privacy review, not just a silent policy edit.
