Affelion Kind — Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

The short version

Affelion Kind is built to be used with nothing to sign up for and nothing tracked. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.

What the app does with what you type or select

Affelion Kind runs fully offline. Every situation, principle, and pre-written message lives inside the app itself. Anything you type to personalize a message, or any message you copy, stays on your device only. Nothing is sent to a server, logged, or shared.

Purchases

Affelion Kind offers one optional one-time in-app purchase ("The alternates"), handled entirely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details — Apple and Google process purchases directly, under their own privacy policies. We do not operate a payment server and do not store purchase records ourselves.

Data we do not collect

We do not require or request your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any device identifiers. We do not use cookies, local storage for tracking, or any persistent tracking technology beyond what is needed to remember your own in-app purchase entitlement on your own device. We do not build user profiles or share data with third parties, because none is collected in the first place.

The safety floor

If you use the in-app link to a crisis helpline directory, that opens a third-party website (findahelpline.com) outside of Affelion Kind, which has its own privacy practices. Affelion Kind does not record that you used this link, does not know your location, and does not store which country you selected — that choice is held only in memory during your session and is gone the moment you close the app.

Children's privacy

Affelion Kind is not directed at children and is rated for ages 12 and up. Because the app collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children, consistent with COPPA and similar regulations.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if we introduce optional account or analytics features in the future — we will update this page and the app's App Store listing before that change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@affelion.com.
