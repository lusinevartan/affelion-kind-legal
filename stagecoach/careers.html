Internships

Academic-credit internships in DC

For students in medical and health programs. Hybrid, about 10 to 15 hours a week, with regular supervision and a real semester project. At our Georgetown office, 3299 K St NW #210.

Medical Operations Intern

Help build and run a new medical service, with our CEO and operations team.

+
Where
Georgetown office, hybrid, with some in-person work
Time
About 10 to 15 hours a week
Term
Semester and dates to be confirmed
Reports to
CEO and operations leaders

Pay and credit

This internship is unpaid by Stagecoach and is built for academic credit. You may get a stipend, grant, scholarship, work-study or other funding from your school or another approved source. We’ll help with any paperwork your school or funder needs.

What you’ll do

  • Help build operations and office workflows
  • Help plan concierge, mobile, executive and event services
  • Check equipment, medicine, vehicles and supplies
  • Track inventory and research equipment and vendors
  • Help with staffing, schedules, credentials and logistics
  • Write checklists, procedures and training materials
  • Research care models, rules, competitors and partners
  • Support event medical plans and reviews afterward
  • Organize information on clients, vendors and partners
  • Make presentations, proposals and reports
  • Join operations and leadership meetings
  • Finish a semester project and present it to leadership

What you’ll learn

  • How a doctor-led mobile medical service runs
  • What concierge, mobile and event care need to work
  • Readiness, logistics and quality checks
  • How to write clear plans, procedures and briefings
  • How medicine, operations, rules and business fit together
  • How to work well with doctors and leaders
  • How to study a problem and suggest real fixes

Who should apply

  • Students in a medical or health program, such as medicine, nursing, PA studies, paramedicine, EMS, public health or healthcare administration
  • EMS experience preferred
  • EMT, AEMT, paramedic, nursing assistant or other certification preferred, not required
  • Strong organizing, research, writing and communication skills
  • Good judgment, reliable and detail-minded
  • Can work on your own and meet deadlines
  • Can keep things private and follow privacy rules
  • Comfortable with Microsoft Office or Google Workspace
  • A driver’s license is a plus

This is an educational role. Interns don’t give unsupervised patient care or medical advice, or do anything beyond a license they already hold. We’ll work with your school on goals, supervision and paperwork. You’re responsible for getting credit approved before you start. The internship doesn’t promise a future job.

How to apply

Email medical@thestagecoachgroup.com with:

  • Your résumé
  • A short note on why you’re interested
  • Your program and expected graduation date
  • Any EMS or healthcare experience
  • Your school-credit or funding needs
  • When you’re available

Office of the Medical Director Intern

Research, protocols and quality work with our Medical Director.

+
Where
Georgetown office, hybrid, with possible in-person observation
Time
About 10 to 15 hours a week
Term
Semester and dates to be confirmed
Reports to
Medical Director and clinical leaders

Pay and credit

This internship is unpaid by Stagecoach and is built for academic credit. You may get a stipend, grant, scholarship, work-study or other funding from your school or another approved source. We’ll help with any paperwork your school or funder needs.

What you’ll do

  • Review research on emergency medicine, EMS and mobile and concierge care
  • Research best practices and professional guidelines
  • Help draft clinical protocols, policies and checklists
  • Support quality, patient safety and clinical oversight work
  • Help build performance measures, audits and case reviews
  • Look at de-identified data for trends and fixes
  • Help with training, skills sessions and simulations
  • Research medicines, equipment, lab tools and new tech
  • Help set clinical standards for concierge, mobile, executive and event care
  • Join clinical meetings, case talks and reviews
  • Observe some training or care, when the law, our policies and the patient allow it
  • Finish a research or quality project and present it to the Medical Director

Possible projects

  • Review the evidence for a new protocol
  • Build a quality plan for mobile medical care
  • Create a training or simulation exercise
  • Set standards for equipment and medicine readiness
  • Research best practice for event or executive care
  • Design ways to measure results for a new program
  • Study patient safety in concierge and mobile medicine

What you’ll learn

  • What a medical director does
  • How protocols are researched, written, approved and checked
  • How to use research to answer a real question
  • The basics of oversight, patient safety and quality
  • How care, rules, operations and admin connect
  • How to explain medical ideas to any audience
  • How to present a project to clinical leaders

Who should apply

  • Students in a medical or health program, such as medicine, nursing, PA studies, paramedicine, EMS, public health or clinical research
  • EMS experience preferred
  • EMT, AEMT, paramedic, nursing assistant or other certification preferred, not required
  • Strong research, analysis, writing and organizing skills
  • Can understand and sum up medical information
  • Good judgment, reliable and detail-minded
  • Can keep things private and follow privacy rules
  • Comfortable with Microsoft Office or Google Workspace

This is an educational role. Interns don’t diagnose, treat, give medical advice, give medicine, or do anything that needs a license unless separately credentialed, allowed and supervised. You may need to complete privacy (HIPAA), infection-control and safety training. Patient information is limited to what the law allows and the work needs, and we use de-identified data whenever we can. The internship doesn’t promise admission to a program or a future job.

How to apply

Email medical@thestagecoachgroup.com with:

  • Your résumé
  • A short note on why you’re interested in the Office of the Medical Director
  • Your program and expected graduation date
  • Any EMS or healthcare experience
  • Your school-credit or funding needs
  • When you’re available
