After hours

When the pediatrician is closed, most families are left with 911 or the ER.

Late at night there is often nowhere else to go. We fill that gap, any hour, with a children’s ER doctor on the line.

How it works

Three Simple Steps

  1. You Call.

    A real person answers, day or night. No phone tree, no waiting for a call back.

  2. We Come to You.

    A clinician checks your child at home, where they feel most comfortable.

  3. We See It Through.

    Treatment at home when it’s safe, a trip to the hospital when it isn’t, and a note to your pediatrician either way.

Working with your pediatrician

    Everything we find and do goes to your pediatrician, so the follow up picks up where we left off.
