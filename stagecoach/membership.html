Why it matters

The ER is not built for you.

In DC, a trip to the ER takes hours and costs a lot. There are few beds for a very big city.

  • 5h 14mThe average ER visit in DC. Almost twice the national average.
  • $2,863The average cost of one ER visit.
  • 350ER beds in all of Washington, DC.
  • 0.05%The share of people DC’s ERs can treat at once: 694,000 residents and about 1 million people by day.

How it works

Three simple steps

  1. You call. A real clinician answers.

    Call or reach us online, any time. No phone tree.

  2. We figure out what you need.

    911, the ER, a telehealth call, a home visit, or safe to wait. We decide together.

  3. If you need us, we come to you.

    An EMT, paramedic, NP, PA, doctor or dentist comes to you. A triple board-certified doctor oversees every visit.

Want us with you?

Great care, right by your side.

You don’t have to wait for an emergency. We can place experienced emergency clinicians with you: at home, at work, or when you travel.

  • A known health condition?
  • Need privacy and fast care?
  • A sick kid while your partner is away?

Real help is on the way. Because your response time matters.

The value

You already pay for the upgrade.

First-class seats. A car service. A concierge. You pay for time, comfort and control. Your health deserves the same. The best time to join is before you need us.
