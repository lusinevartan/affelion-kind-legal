Affelion Halo Support

Getting a clear reading. Rest your fingertip lightly over the back camera and the flash together. Pressing hard blocks the light. Keep your hand still, ideally with your elbow resting on something, in a comfortably lit room, with warm hands. The flash may feel slightly warm; that is normal. If Halo says "Let's try that again", that try did not use your free check-in.

What the colors mean. Every color comes from a number through a fixed set of rules. Tap a number on your portrait, or open the full breakdown, to see which reading made which color. The same numbers always give the same color.

Free and Pro. First scan free, then one free scan a week. Halo Pro is unlimited. To restore Pro on a new phone: Settings → Restore Purchases. To cancel: your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

Your data. Check-ins stay on your phone. Settings → Delete all data removes them. See the Privacy Policy: https://www.affelion.com/halo/privacy and the Terms of Use: https://www.affelion.com/halo/terms
