Affelion Halo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Affelion Halo ("Halo") is an app made by Affelion LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what Halo does with information about you. In short: your check-ins stay on your phone, there is no account, and we do not track you.

What Halo uses, and where it stays

Camera. The front camera finds your outline and takes one photo for your portrait. The back camera and flash read your pulse through your fingertip. Camera frames are processed on your phone and thrown away. The one photo and your outline are saved inside the app on your phone so your portrait can be shown again.

Microphone. Halo listens for about five seconds to measure the pitch and steadiness of your voice. The sound is analyzed in memory on your phone. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Nothing you say is written down.

Your check-ins. Halo saves the numbers from each check-in (approximate pulse, how much it varies, voice pitch and steadiness), the color settings those numbers produce, your breathing practice results and your app settings. All of this is stored only on your phone.

Photos. Halo saves a picture to your photo library only when you tap Save, and shares one only when you tap Share and choose where it goes. Halo does not read your photo library.

Sasha, the guide. In this version Sasha's answers are made on your phone from your own numbers. Your questions and numbers are not sent to us or to any AI company.

We do not receive your photos, your voice, your measurements or your check-in history. We cannot see them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details.

To know whether you have Halo Pro, and to let you restore it on a new phone, Halo uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives your purchase records (which product, when, and its status) and a random ID created by the app. That ID is not your name, email or Apple or Google account. We use these records to unlock Pro and to see how many people subscribe in total. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we do not do

No account or sign-up. No advertising. No tracking across other apps or websites. No analytics or crash-reporting tools. We do not sell or share your information. Halo does not use HealthKit or Health Connect.

Our website

Our public privacy and support pages are hosted on Replit. Replit may process visit and connection information, such as IP addresses, browser and device details, and pages viewed, and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy policy. See https://replit.com/privacy-policy.

Deleting your information

Settings → Delete all data removes every check-in, photo, portrait, practice session and setting from the app. Deleting the app removes them too. Your Pro status and your free-scan week are kept by the App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat, so deleting data does not cancel a subscription. To cancel, use your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

To ask us to delete purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us (below) from any address and include the date and store of your purchase.

Children

Halo is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because check-ins stay on the phone, we do not have any child's check-in data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Apart from RevenueCat's purchase records, we hold none. Email us to make a request.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
