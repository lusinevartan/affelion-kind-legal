# Affelion Halo Support

*To publish at https://www.affelion.com/halo/support.*

**Getting a clear reading.** Rest your fingertip lightly over the back camera and the flash together.
Pressing hard blocks the light. Keep your hand still, ideally with your elbow resting on something,
in a comfortably lit room, with warm hands. The flash may feel slightly warm; that is normal. If
Halo says "Let's try that again", that try did not use your free check-in.

**What the colors mean.** Every color comes from a number through a fixed set of rules. Tap a number
on your portrait, or open the full breakdown, to see which reading made which color. The same
numbers always give the same color.

**Free and Pro.** First scan free, then one free scan a week. Halo Pro is unlimited. To restore Pro
on a new phone: Settings → Restore Purchases. To cancel: your Apple ID or Google Play subscription
settings.

**Your data.** Check-ins stay on your phone. Settings → Delete all data removes them. See the
Privacy Policy: https://www.affelion.com/halo/privacy

**Contact.** hello@affelion.com. Tell us your phone model and app version. Please don't send health
records, passwords or card details.

*Affelion Halo is for wellness and self-reflection. It is not a medical device and does not
diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Talk to a doctor about any health concern.*
