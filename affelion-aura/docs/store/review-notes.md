Affelion Halo is a one-minute wellness and self-reflection check-in. It turns an approximate pulse
and a few seconds of voice into a color portrait around a black-and-white photo of the user.

NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE
The app does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent or monitor any condition and makes no medical
claims. The portrait, the full breakdown, How it works and Settings all show: "Affelion Halo is for
wellness and self-reflection. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or
prevent any medical condition. Talk to a doctor about any health concern." The pulse is labeled
approximate. The app does not use HealthKit.

HOW TO DO A CHECK-IN (no account needed)
1. Open the app, read the three intro pages, tap Begin scan. Allow the camera and microphone.
2. Face: look at the front camera. The app finds your outline and takes one photo.
3. Fingertip: cover the BACK camera and the flash together with one fingertip and hold still for
   about 30 to 40 seconds. The flash turns on so light shines through the fingertip; that is how
   the pulse is read.
4. Voice: say a few words for about five seconds. The sound is analyzed in memory for pitch and
   steadiness and is never recorded or saved. "Skip voice" is available.
5. The portrait appears with one of 20 colors. If the reading was not clear, the app says
   "We couldn't get a clear reading. Try again..." and that try does not use the free check-in.
A simulator cannot read a pulse (it has no flash or fingertip signal), so please test on an iPhone.

HOW THE COLORS ARE MADE
Colors come from the measurements (approximate pulse, beat-to-beat variation, voice pitch and
steadiness) through a fixed rulebook. The same numbers always give the same color. Tapping a number
on the portrait, or opening the full breakdown, shows which reading made which color. The app makes
no claims about energy fields, the future or anything supernatural.

PRIVACY
Camera frames and audio are processed on the device and discarded. The photo, outline and
measurements are stored only on the device; "Delete all data" in Settings removes them. No account,
no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple, with RevenueCat used to check
subscription status. Sasha, the in-app guide, is labeled as an AI; in 1.0 her answers are built on
the device from the user's own numbers and nothing is sent to a server.

SUBSCRIPTIONS AND THE PAYWALL
Free: the first check-in is free, then one free check-in a week, plus one breathing practice.
Halo Pro: yearly (with a 7-day free trial for eligible users) and monthly auto-renewing
subscriptions, and a one-time lifetime purchase.
- Reaching the paywall: it opens by itself two seconds after the first portrait. It can also be
  opened any time from the portrait ("See the full breakdown"), from Home when the free check-in is
  used ("Unlock scans"), or from Settings ("See plans").
- The paywall shows each price and billing period from the store, the trial terms, the renewal
  terms, Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. "Not now ✕" closes it and returns to
  the portrait.
- Restoring: Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. It needs no account.
- The lifetime purchase is behind "Prefer to pay once?" on the paywall.
