# 60-second demo video: shot list and script

For App Review (linked in the Review Notes) and reusable as raw footage for the launch reel. Record
on your iPhone with the TestFlight build, using iOS Screen Recording (Control Center → Screen
Recording; long-press it and turn **Microphone on** so your voice step is audible).

## Before you record

- Fresh install from TestFlight (delete and reinstall) so onboarding and the free check-in show.
- Use a **sandbox tester** in Settings → App Store → Sandbox Account, so the paywall shows real
  prices without charging you.
- Do Not Disturb on. Battery above 50%. Brightness up. Sit at a table in a comfortably lit room,
  warm hands, elbow resting on the table.
- Do one practice run first so you know the rhythm. Aim for a magenta or violet result only if it
  happens naturally; don't fake numbers.

## Shot list (screen recording, one continuous take, about 60 seconds)

| Time | On screen | What you do | Optional voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Onboarding page 1 | Swipe through the three pages, tap **Begin scan** | "This is Affelion Halo. A one-minute check-in." |
| 0:05–0:08 | Camera and microphone prompts | Tap **Allow** on both | "It asks for the camera and microphone. Everything stays on the phone." |
| 0:08–0:15 | Face step, oval | Center your face until it says Hold still | "First, the front camera finds my outline and takes one photo." |
| 0:15–0:50 | Fingertip step | Turn the phone, cover the back camera and flash with one fingertip, hold still until the voice step starts (fast-forward this part in editing if you want, but keep it uncut for App Review) | "Now a fingertip over the back camera and flash. The flash shines through my finger to read an approximate pulse." |
| 0:50–0:56 | Voice step | Say: "Hi, this is my one-minute check-in." | "Five seconds of voice, for pitch and steadiness. Nothing is recorded." |
| 0:56–1:00 | Portrait appears | Hold on it for two seconds | "And this is my color." |
| 1:00–1:10 | Paywall opens by itself | Point at Restore Purchases, Terms and Privacy; tap **Not now ✕** | "Pro is offered once. Not now takes me back." |
| 1:10–1:15 | Portrait | Tap number 1 on the portrait to show which reading made the color | "Every color traces to a number." |

It runs a little over 60 seconds with the paywall; that's fine for App Review. Trim to 60 for any
social use.

## After recording

1. Photos → the recording → Edit → trim the start and end only (no cuts in the middle for App
   Review).
2. Upload as **Unlisted** on YouTube (or a shared iCloud or Google Drive link set to "anyone with
   the link"). Open the link in a private browser window to confirm it plays without signing in.
3. Paste the link into `review-notes.md` where it says [LINK TO VIDEO], or send it to Claude.
