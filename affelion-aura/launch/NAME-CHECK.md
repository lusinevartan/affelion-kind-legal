## 4. Verdict
- **As an App Store name, "Affelion Halo – Color Check-In" looks available.** No app uses that exact name, and Apple requires unique names, not unique words. Final proof is reserving the name in App Store Connect, which only Lusine can do.
- **"Halo" alone is not safe as a brand.** It's heavily used and registered in class 9 (Microsoft, ScreenZen and others), and in wellness/class 44 there are live registrations (Solera, Centene) plus pending ones from Amazon and Alethra covering app-delivered wellness and heart-rate monitoring, very close to what this app does. Always pair it with the house mark: "Affelion Halo". Never ship or market a standalone "Halo" logo or wordmark.
- **Risk level (my read, not legal advice): moderate.** "Affelion" is distinctive and makes the combined name clearly different, but a big owner (Amazon, if it revives its application) could still object to "Halo" for wellness software. Before spending on branding, Lusine should (a) reserve the name in App Store Connect and Play Console, and (b) consider a low-cost attorney knock-out search or file "AFFELION HALO" in class 9 (and possibly 44).
