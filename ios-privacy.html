How Kind uses your data.

Affelion Kind for iOS · Updated October 15, 2026

Kind and your words

Affelion LLC provides Kind. Its wording is built into the app and works offline. There is no Kind account or ad software. Kind does not send your chosen situation, context or edited words to us or to an AI service.

Kind keeps your choices and drafts in app memory. It keeps no history of them. Discarding a draft clears it from the app. A full app restart clears the draft too. Leaving the app in the background does not clear it. Your keyboard, dictation and device features follow your device settings.

On your device

Kind saves whether you have seen the welcome. That setting stays until app data is cleared, subject to your device's backup rules. Kind does not erase device backups or promise that removing the app removes all device records.

Your help country stays in the current session. Kind may suggest it from your device language or region, not GPS. You can change it. Kind does not save that choice or log which situations you open.

Copy puts your chosen words on the clipboard. It does not send a message. Other apps, clipboard sync or device features may keep or share that text. Clearing a draft does not clear the clipboard. What you paste elsewhere follows that service's rules.

The store

Apple handles prices, purchases and restores. Kind checks the price and your access with Apple when it opens and when access may have changed. These checks may need internet access. You do not have to buy to use the free words.

Kind receives purchase details on your device to check access. The checks do not include your situation or message text. We do not receive your card details. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its rules. Removing Kind does not delete those records.

Links you choose

Outside websites open when you choose a link. The helpline link can include your chosen country. It does not include your draft or GPS location. The site can see your visit and normal connection details, such as your IP address.

Our public legal pages use GitHub Pages. Those pages and other sites have their own privacy rules. See GitHub's privacy statement.

When you email us

If you email hello@affelion.com, we receive your sender name, address, message and files. We use them only to handle your request. You may send your device type and app version. We may ask for an order reference to check a purchase.

Google hosts our support mail. Only Kind's owner reads and handles support. We keep messages and files until the owner deletes them by hand. Google's own rules apply to the mail it processes. We do not promise that deleting mail removes all provider copies at once.

Please do not send private chats, health records, passwords or card details. Support is for the app, purchases, privacy and product safety. It is not an emergency or health care service.

How we use data

We do not use support mail for ads, marketing lists or user profiles. Kind has no added analytics or tracking tools. Apple may process store and device data under its own rules.

Your choice

Email us to ask about access to your support data, a correction or deletion. We may need to check that the request is yours. Your rights depend on the law that applies. For data held by Apple or another service, contact that service too.

Kind does not ask for your birth date or ID. A store age rating does not mean Kind checks your age. We will update this notice when our practices change.
